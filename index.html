For women over 20, a monthly breast self-exam is your most powerful first step. Know your body — know when something changes.

02 / 12

Know
The
Signs

If you notice any of these, don't wait — contact a specialist immediately:

  • !A lump or thickening in the breast or underarm
  • !Change in the size, shape, or appearance of the breast
  • !Skin changes — dimpling, redness, or thickening
  • !Nipple retraction or unusual discharge
  • !Persistent pain in one specific area
